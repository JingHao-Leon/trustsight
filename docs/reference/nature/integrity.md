# Integrity and Verification: Rule Nature

Mechanics live on the [Integrity system page](../rules/integrity.md).

## Where it comes from

A PKGBUILD's promise to a user is `sha256sums` (or a signature): download
this, and it will match. The integrity family is about breaking that promise
in a way that still looks intact. The rules cover `SKIP` and placeholder
sums, sums generated by the build itself, `md5sums`/`sha1sums` where a
stronger algorithm belongs, and the source URL mutating after verification.

This is why the checksum rules sit close to the fetch rules in importance:
an unverified source makes fetch-and-execute far more dangerous, because the
reviewer can no longer reason about what was fetched.

## What a finding means

- The finding says the verification is defeated or absent, and where. A
  `SKIP` or a freshly-computed sum means the tarball's contents are not
  vouched for by the recipe at all.
- Integrity findings are strong amplifiers: a weakened checksum beside a
  dynamic source is a much higher risk than either alone.
- Weak-algorithm findings are about hygiene meetings a real bar; read them as
  "this verification will not stop a substitution".
